`PatternBand` lays the Elemen Grafis strip into a layout — the signature JUP decoration and the only one.

```jsx
<PatternBand orientation="row" tone="white" opacity={0.22} height={90} align="right" />
```

Use `column` for portrait and vertical formats. Keep it to an edge, never behind body copy, and never over half the layout. Tones: `ink`, `white`, `green`, `blue`.
