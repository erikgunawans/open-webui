`SectionHeading` opens every major block on a JUP surface. The eyebrow's trailing rule is the identity's quiet signature — keep it.

```jsx
<SectionHeading
	eyebrow="Portofolio"
	title="Utilitas yang menopang Jakarta"
	lead="Lima lini usaha, satu tujuan: energi dan utilitas yang berkelanjutan bagi warga Jakarta."
/>
```
