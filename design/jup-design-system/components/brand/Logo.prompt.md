`Logo` places an approved JUP lockup. Never re-typeset, recolour or stretch it.

```jsx
<Logo variant="primary" height={44} />
<Logo variant="primary-white" height={36} base="../assets" />
```

Horizontal media take `primary`; vertical media (banner, x-banner, umbul-umbul) take `secondary`. Below ~5cm printed, drop the JAKARTA UTILITAS line and the Jakpro endorsement — that is `compact`.
