## Named non-coverage — what this plan does *not* prove

- Gas figures are **potential revenue, not margin**. B-to-G tariffs are set by government; margin is policy-dependent.
- The 15,4 GWh/yr WTP energy load is **derived from capacity (150 lps × ~3,25 kWh/m³)**, not metered.
- Market sizing (TAM) is **illustrative** — RUED capacity targets × industry unit costs, not a bottom-up study.
- Solar offset % depends on a **load-profile study** that has not been done.
- Governance-layer costs are a **team estimate**, not vendor quotes.
